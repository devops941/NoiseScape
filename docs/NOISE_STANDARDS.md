# Environmental Noise Standards Reference

This document outlines the standard regulatory ambient air quality standards for noise prescribed by environmental regulatory bodies (e.g., Central Pollution Control Board - CPCB India, EPA, and WHO guidelines).

---

## 1. Ambient Air Quality Standards in Respect of Noise

| Area Code | Category of Area / Zone | Day Limit in dB(A) Leq* | Night Limit in dB(A) Leq* |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **A** | **Industrial Area** | 75 dB(A) | 70 dB(A) |
| **B** | **Commercial Area** | 65 dB(A) | 55 dB(A) |
| **C** | **Residential Area** | 55 dB(A) | 45 dB(A) |
| **D** | **Silence Zone** | 50 dB(A) | 40 dB(A) |

### Notes:
1. **Day time** is reckoned from 06:00 to 22:00 (6:00 AM to 10:00 PM).
2. **Night time** is reckoned from 22:00 to 06:00 (10:00 PM to 6:00 AM).
3. **Silence Zone** includes areas comprising not less than 100 metres around hospitals, educational institutions, courts, religious places, or any other area declared as such by the competent authority.
4. `dB(A) Leq` denotes the time-weighted average of the sound level in decibels on scale A, which is related to human hearing.

---

## 2. Severity Classification Used in the System

- **Safe (Normal)**: Sound level is $\le$ the permissible threshold for the zone and current time.
- **Warning**: Sound level exceeds the threshold by $1$ to $10\text{ dB(A)}$.
- **Critical (Breach)**: Sound level exceeds the threshold by $> 10\text{ dB(A)}$.
